The name

Why Dingo?

The dingo is Australia's wild dog. Nobody feeds it and nobody tames it. It covers hundreds of miles of brutal country on its own and still eats every night.

It's also what the brothers in this family have called each other our whole lives. So when it was time to put a name on the door, there was no debate.

We run like the dingo: lean, smart, and built for the long haul. Your freight's in good hands.
