Open seats

Two ways to run

You can run as a team or run solo. Here is the difference, so you know what you are signing up for.

Team drivers

You and a co driver share one truck. While one of you drives, the other sleeps, then you switch. The truck keeps moving, so you turn loads faster and get more of them done.

You driveCo driver drivesFuel and stops

Have a co driver? Bring them on board with you. Open to teaming up? Tell us when you call.

Solo drivers

One driver, one truck. By law you can drive 11 hours, then you have to stop. Solo trucks take the loads that have room for that in the schedule.

DrivingOff duty and rest

Tell us if you want OTR or regional and we will go from there.

Why teams matter here: most of our U.S. Mail loads have to run straight through. Think Omaha, Nebraska to New Jersey with no overnight stop. Only a team truck can make that trip on time. Bars show one 24 hour day and are for illustration.
